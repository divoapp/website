Common questions

How do I split a bill?

Tap ＋, then New split. Scan the receipt, choose a photo of it, add the items yourself, or type just the total. Add the people who were there, pick who paid, then tap Finish split so everyone can see what they owe.

What's a Tab?

A Tab is one running balance across several splits, for a trip, a weekend, or roommates. Everyone settles once at the end instead of after every bill. Tap ＋, then Start a Tab.

How do I mark that I've paid someone back?

Open the split and tap Paid. The person you paid taps Received. Once everyone has paid, the split settles on its own a little later, or the person who paid can tap Settle up.

Divo keeps track of who owes what. It doesn't move money: you pay each other however you usually do.

How do I add a friend?

Tap ＋, then Add a friend, and search for their username. You can also share your profile from your profile page: friends can scan your QR code or open your link.

How does receipt scanning work?

Divo asks before it reads a receipt with AI. If you agree, the photo is read by Anthropic's Claude to pull out the items, tax and tip, and isn't kept. You can turn this off any time in Settings → Privacy and security. See the privacy policy for the details.

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password?. We'll email a code to the address on your account. If you signed up without an email, write to us from any address with your username and we'll help.

How do I cancel Premium?

Go to your profile, tap Manage Premium, then Cancel subscription. Premium is billed through the App Store, so you can also cancel in your iPhone's Settings under your Apple Account and Subscriptions. Premium stays on until the end of the period you paid for.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings from your profile and tap Delete account at the bottom. This permanently deletes your account and your data. If you'd rather we did it, email us from the address on your account.
